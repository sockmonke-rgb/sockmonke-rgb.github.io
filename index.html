Lunabara

A 3D moon colony you watch

Seven capybaras in bubble helmets live on the Shackleton crater rim, and you follow their day through the colony's cameras: drilling cores, building a fusion reactor, unloading landers from Earth and soaking in yuzu baths with Earth on the horizon. It runs itself, and you can lend a hand at any station.

Astrobara

Turn-based lunar survival

A herd of capybaras holds a colony on the crater rim, and the enemy is the night. A lunar day is 29 turns and only 15 are lit, so everything you build in the light is a bill that comes due in the dark. The game is deciding what to switch off.

Ink Strike 笔阵

Chinese handwriting against the clock

Characters drift down toward your desk, and you write each one before it lands. Stroke order and direction both count. Each wave shares a radical, so you start seeing it everywhere. About 180 characters from HSK 1 to 4, with a no-clock mode for practice.

Play Source code

Silicon Cycle

Tower defense on a chip fab floor

Bear forces walk the fab floor toward your book, and you hold them off with positions across the chip stack, from memory and analog to EUV lithography. The boom-and-bust cycle swings prices under you, and every wave you hold a position, it pays more. Clear wave 30 to confirm the thesis.

Spectre Lab

An explorer for the Spectre tile

The Spectre, found in 2023, is a single tile that covers the plane but never in a repeating pattern. Pan across an endless tiling, tap any tile to see where it sits in the hierarchy, paint your own design in the tile studio and export it as a PNG or PDF.
